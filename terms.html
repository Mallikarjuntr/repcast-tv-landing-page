Last updated: 16 October 2026

In short: Spotter is a coach, not a doctor. Exercise within your limits, clear the space around you, and stop if anything hurts.

Your health comes first

  • Spotter gives general fitness guidance. It is not medical advice and does not replace a doctor or physiotherapist.
  • Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise routine, especially if you are pregnant, have a health condition, an injury, or haven't exercised for a while.
  • Stop straight away if you feel pain, dizziness, shortness of breath or discomfort.
  • Rep counts and form notes come from a camera and can be wrong. Use your own judgement.

Keep your space safe

  • Clear enough room to move, away from furniture, stairs, pets and children.
  • Place your phone where it can't fall or be stepped on, and keep cables out of the way.
  • You are responsible for how and where you exercise.
